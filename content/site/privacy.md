*Last updated: November 2026*

## In short

Xiangqi Forum is a **static website**. It does not use cookies, does not run any JavaScript, and does not include advertising or third-party tracking scripts.

## What the site itself collects

Nothing. The pages contain text, images and links only. There are no forms, accounts or comments.

## What the hosting provider may collect

Like every website, the infrastructure that serves the pages (a content delivery network) processes technical information — such as IP address, browser type and the requested page — to deliver the content and keep it secure. This is standard server logging outside our control.

## Links to other sites

Some pages link to [Xiangqi 3D](https://xiangqi3d.online) and [Chess 3D](https://chess3d.online). Those games have their own data handling: they use an anonymous account code stored on your device and a display name you choose. This policy covers only xiangqi.forum.

## Changes

If this changes — for example if analytics are added — this page will be updated before they go live.
