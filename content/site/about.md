**Xiangqi Forum** is a free learning resource for two board games: **xiangqi (Chinese chess)** and **chess**. It exists for one reason: to make it easy to learn the rules, understand the ideas and then play.

## What you will find here

- **Step-by-step guides** to the rules, pieces, notation, openings, tactics, checkmates and endgames of [xiangqi](/xiangqi/) and [chess](/chess/).
- **Diagrams checked by a rules engine.** Move dots and checkmate positions are generated from the same rules code that runs the two games, so a diagram cannot show an illegal move or a false checkmate.
- **Explainers** such as [how ELO ratings work](/elo-rating-explained/).
- **Pointers to play**: [Xiangqi 3D](https://xiangqi3d.online) and [Chess 3D](https://chess3d.online), two browser games with a 3D board, a real two-button clock and a rating ladder.

## Editorial approach

- Guides are written in plain English for beginners and improving players.
- Where a rule or a piece of history is uncertain or varies by organisation (for example, repetition rules in xiangqi), the page says so.
- Piece values and strategic advice are rules of thumb, not laws.
- Pages carry an "Updated" date, and corrections are made as soon as an error is found.

## Relationship to the games

This site is the companion to Xiangqi 3D and Chess 3D and links to them. The games are free to play in a browser; see [play online](/play/) for what they offer.

## Languages

The guides are currently in English. The Xiangqi 3D game has an English and Chinese interface.
