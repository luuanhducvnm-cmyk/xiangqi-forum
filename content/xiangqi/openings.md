An opening is how you arrange your pieces in the first 8–10 moves. Xiangqi has dozens of named systems, but beginners need only five ideas. Moves are given in [WXF notation](/xiangqi/notation/): `C2=5` means "cannon on file 2 goes sideways to file 5".

## 1. Central Cannon (中炮, zhōng pào)

**1. C2=5.** Red's cannon moves to the central file and immediately aims at the soldier in front of Black's general. It sets up threats against the centre and prepares horse and chariot development.

{{img:xq-central-cannon|Central Cannon: Red's cannon on file 2 has moved to the centre (C2=5).}}

**Idea:** pressure on the central file, fast development, and attacking chances against the black soldier on file 5. It is the standard weapon for players who want an active attacking game. See the [cannon page](/xiangqi/pieces/cannon/) for how screens work.

## 2. Screen Horse defence (屏風馬, píngfēng mǎ)

The most common reply: **1...H8+7 2.H2+3 H2+3**. Black develops both horses to cover the centre, and the soldiers on files 3 and 7 (the "screen") protect them.

{{img:xq-screen-horse|The Screen Horse structure after 1.C2=5 H8+7 2.H2+3 H2+3.}}

**Idea:** a solid defence. The two horses protect each other and the central soldier. After the horses come out, Black usually develops the chariots and later challenges the central cannon.

## 3. Same-direction vs opposite cannons

Black can answer the Central Cannon with a cannon of his own:

- **Same-direction cannons (順炮, shùn pào):** 1.C2=5 C8=5. Both cannons end up on the central file from the same side of the board.
- **Opposite cannons (列炮, liè pào):** 1.C2=5 C2=5. The cannons come from opposite sides.

Both lead to sharp, tactical play with early exchanges — good practice for beginners who like tactics.

## 4. Elephant opening (飛相局, fēi xiàng jú)

**1. E3+5.** Red brings an elephant to the centre. It is a quiet, defensive move that keeps the position flexible and waits for the opponent to commit.

## 5. Pawn (soldier) opening (仙人指路, "the immortal shows the way")

**1. P7+1.** Red advances the soldier on file 7. It is a waiting move that keeps both horses and cannons flexible; Black must decide how to respond before Red reveals the plan.

## 6. Cross-palace cannon (過宮炮)

**1. C2=6.** The cannon goes to file 6, next to the central file, instead of straight onto it. A flexible, positional alternative to the Central Cannon that keeps more options for the other pieces.

## How to choose

| If you like… | Try as Red… | Try as Black… |
|---|---|---|
| Direct attack | Central Cannon (C2=5) | Opposite cannons |
| Solid play | Elephant opening (E3+5) | Screen Horse defence |
| Flexibility | Soldier opening (P7+1) | Same-direction cannons |

## Principles to follow in any opening

- **Develop the chariots** — move the horse and cannon out of the way to open their files ([chariot page](/xiangqi/pieces/chariot/)).
- **Do not move the same piece twice** unless there is a threat.
- **Keep the horse legs free** (see [the horse](/xiangqi/pieces/horse/)).
- **Do not push soldiers** without a plan.
- **Watch the centre file**; exposed generals lose quickly.
