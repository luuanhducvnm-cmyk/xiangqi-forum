The **Central Cannon** (中炮, *zhōng pào*) is the most popular way to start a xiangqi game. Red plays **1. C2=5**: the cannon on file 2 slides sideways to the central file, directly in front of its own general.

In [WXF notation](/xiangqi/notation/) `C2=5` means "the cannon on file 2 moves horizontally to file 5". If you are new to the notation, read that page first; it takes two minutes.

## What the first move does

{{img:xq-cc-target|After 1.C2=5 the cannon is on the central file; the highlighted points show where it can go, including a capture on the central Black soldier.}}

A cannon captures by jumping over exactly one piece. On the central file the cannon has Red's own soldier directly in front of it, and that soldier serves as the **screen**. Behind the screen, the first piece on the file is Black's central soldier, so the cannon already *threatens to capture it*. You can see the capture point among the highlighted squares in the diagram.

That single move therefore does three things:

1. It **attacks the central soldier**, forcing Black to react or lose material.
2. It puts a piece on the **file of Black's general**, where later tactics (cannon checks, horse jumps with check) happen.
3. It frees file 2 so the chariot on file 1 can move there quickly.

## Black's main replies

### Screen Horse defence (屏風馬)

Black's most common answer is **1...H8+7**, followed by **H2+3**. Each horse guards the central soldier, because a horse on file 7 or file 3 (in Black's own file numbering) is exactly one jump away from it. The soldiers on files 3 and 7 shield the horses from direct attacks, which is why it is called a "screen".

{{img:xq-screen-horse|The Screen Horse structure: both Black horses protect the central soldier.}}

### Same-direction cannons (順炮)

**1...C8=5.** Black also puts a cannon on the central file, from the same side of the board as Red's. The central soldiers are now attacked from both directions and the game gets tactical quickly.

{{img:xq-cc-same|Same-direction cannons: both cannons stand on the central file, starting from the same side of the board.}}

### Opposite cannons (列炮)

**1...C2=5.** Black answers with the cannon from the opposite side. Both sides attack the same central file; the game is sharp, with early trades of horses and chariots.

{{img:xq-cc-opposite|Opposite cannons: Black's cannon comes from the other wing.}}

There are other defences, but these three cover most of the games you will meet online.

## Plans for Red

Whatever Black chooses, Red's plan is the same:

- **Develop the horses** (for example 2. H2+3 and H8+7) so they support the central cannon.
- **Bring the chariots out** (R1=2 and R9=8). A chariot that stays in the corner does nothing; chariots are the strongest pieces.
- **Keep the cannon on the central file** as long as it is useful. Do not move it away just because a threat appears; check whether the threat is real.
- **Count attackers and defenders** on the central file before capturing there. A free soldier is not free if your cannon is lost afterwards.
- Consider advancing a soldier on file 3 or 7 to open lines for horses and chariots.

## Common mistakes with the Central Cannon

- **Blocking your own horse.** A horse is hobbled by a piece next to it (the "leg"). Check the [horse page](/xiangqi/pieces/horse/) before pushing soldiers in front of horses.
- **Forgetting the flying general.** Opening the central file can leave the two generals facing each other. See the [flying general rule](/xiangqi/flying-general-rule/).
- **Trading chariots too early.** Chariots are worth about 9 points; do not swap them for a horse and a soldier without a plan. See [piece values](/xiangqi/piece-values/).
- **Capturing without checking recaptures.** Always ask: what can capture my piece after I capture?

## Practise it

The best way to learn the Central Cannon is to play it. Open a game against the Royal AI at the easiest level and try the plan above: central cannon, two horses, two chariots.
