A xiangqi board looks unfamiliar at first, but it has only a few parts. Learn them once and every diagram in this site will make sense.

## The grid

The board has **9 vertical lines** and **10 horizontal lines**. Pieces stand on the **intersections** (points), so there are 9 × 10 = **90 points**. Chess squares do not exist in xiangqi.

{{img:xq-board|The empty board. The two palaces are highlighted; the river runs across the middle.}}

## The river

Between the fifth and sixth ranks is an empty band, the **river** (楚河漢界, "Chu river, Han border" — a reference to the war that inspired the game's theme). Vertical lines are broken there, so pieces move across it but the board is clearly split into two halves.

- **Elephants** can never cross the river.
- **Soldiers** gain sideways movement after crossing it.
- A **horse** or **chariot** is not restricted, but pieces beyond the river are more exposed.

## The palaces

Each side has a 3×3 zone marked with two crossing diagonals: the **palace** (九宮). The **general** and the **advisors** may never leave their palace. Nothing else is restricted to it — enemy pieces may enter and attack inside.

## Where each piece starts

Red is at the bottom. From left to right on Red's back rank (ranks counted from Red's side):

| Rank | Pieces |
|---|---|
| 1st (back) | Chariot, Horse, Elephant, Advisor, **General**, Advisor, Elephant, Horse, Chariot |
| 3rd | Cannon on the 2nd and 8th files (from the left) |
| 4th | Soldiers on the 1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th and 9th files |

Black mirrors this at the top of the board.

## File numbers

Files are numbered **from each player's own right-hand side**, 1 to 9:

- Red counts 1–9 from **right to left** as seen from Red's chair. In diagrams, Red's files appear as Chinese numerals (一 to 九) along the bottom edge: nine on the left, one on the right.
- Black counts 1–9 from Black's right, which is the **left** side of the board in a normal diagram. Black's files appear as digits along the top edge.

That is why the general always stands on **file 5** for both players, and why the [notation](/xiangqi/notation/) `C2=5` means "the cannon on file 2 moves to the centre file".

## Setting up a physical set

1. Place the board between the players, the river parallel to the edges.
2. Put the generals on the centre points of the back ranks, advisors on each side, then elephants, horses, chariots in the corners.
3. Place cannons on the points just in front of the horses, and soldiers on the marked points (usually shown with small cross marks).
4. Red plays first.

In [Xiangqi 3D](https://xiangqi3d.online) all of this is done for you, and the board turns so that your pieces are always at the bottom.
