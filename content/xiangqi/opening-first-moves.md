Red moves first in xiangqi, and the first move sets the style of the game. Below are four classic starts with their Chinese names. This is an overview of **ideas**, not a complete repertoire. **Every diagram was produced by playing the move in the Xiangqi 3D rules engine**, so the positions are legal. If you are new to the notation, read [xiangqi notation](/xiangqi/notation/) first.

## 1. Central Cannon (中炮), 1.C2=5

Red's cannon jumps to the central file and aims at Black's central soldier and general file. It is direct and aggressive, and it is one of the most popular first moves. See the full guide to the [Central Cannon opening](/xiangqi/central-cannon-opening/).

{{img:xq-central-cannon|Central Cannon: 1.C2=5, the cannon aims at the centre.}}

## 2. Horse opening (起马局), 1.H2+3

Red develops a horse first. This move commits to nothing, so it can transpose into many structures, including the Central Cannon later.

{{img:xl-horse|Horse opening: 1.H2+3, a flexible developing move.}}

## 3. Flying Elephant opening (飞相局), 1.E3+5

Red moves an elephant to the central point of its side of the river. The elephant protects the centre and the cannon and makes the position solid before any attack starts.

{{img:xl-elephant|Flying Elephant opening: 1.E3+5.}}

A common type of reply is for Black to take the centre with a cannon, for example **1...C2=5**:

{{img:xl-elephant-cc|1.E3+5 C2=5: Black takes the central file with a cannon.}}

## 4. Pawn opening (仙人指路), 1.P7+1

Red pushes the soldier on file 7 one step forward. The Chinese name means "the immortal points the way". It does not reveal Red's plan, which is why it is popular with players who like to react to the opponent.

{{img:xl-pawn|Pawn opening: 1.P7+1, the soldier on file 7 steps forward.}}

Black may answer 1...E3+5, a solid developing reply:

## How to choose

| Style | First move |
|---|---|
| Direct attack | Central Cannon |
| Flexible, keeps options | Horse opening |
| Solid, defend first | Elephant opening |
| Wait and see | Pawn opening |

No first move wins by force; what matters is understanding the structures that arise. Learn the [piece values](/xiangqi/piece-values/) and avoid the [beginner mistakes](/xiangqi/beginner-mistakes/), then practise on [Xiangqi 3D](https://xiangqi3d.online).
