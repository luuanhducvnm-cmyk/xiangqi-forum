The endgame starts when most pieces have left the board. In xiangqi this phase is subtle: the general's palace and the defenders (advisors and elephants) make winning harder than it looks. These are **principles**, not memorised positions — use them as guidance.

## 1. The defenders matter

- **Advisors and elephants make fortresses.** A pair of each is difficult to break with a single attacker, so being a piece ahead does not always mean a win.
- **Trade defenders, not just pieces.** Converting a small advantage usually means removing the defenders first.
- **Do not trade your own defenders away** when you are the one defending.

## 2. The soldiers become kings

- Soldiers across the river can move sideways and are your main winning chances.
- **Connected soldiers** (side by side) are stronger than isolated ones.
- A soldier at the edge of the palace restricts the opposing general and supports chariot or horse checks ([the soldier](/xiangqi/pieces/soldier/)).
- **Do not rush soldiers into the palace** without support; they can be captured by an advisor.

## 3. Pieces in the endgame

| Piece | Endgame strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|
| Chariot | Dominates open files; strongest attacker | Needs a partner to mate against solid defence |
| Horse | Fine in sparse positions; can fork general and piece | Blocked legs, slow |
| Cannon | Strong when there are screens | Weak in empty positions |
| Soldier | Can win with support | Slow; can be traded off |

## 4. The general in the endgame

- The general can step forward within its palace to control a file or attack an enemy soldier.
- Never allow the generals to face each other ([flying general](/xiangqi/rules/)) — in endgames this rule can cost a piece instantly.
- Keep your general off the files where an enemy chariot or cannon can attack.

## 5. Converting an advantage

1. **Count material, then count defenders.** If the defender has both advisors and both elephants, you will need more than one extra piece.
2. **Trade pieces, not soldiers.** Keep soldiers for the final attack.
3. **Keep the initiative.** Make threats; do not give the defender time to regroup.
4. **Aim for a mating net.** Look at the [mating patterns](/xiangqi/checkmate-patterns/) and see which one you can reach.

## 6. Saving a worse endgame

- Keep the defenders at home and the general safe.
- Aim for **perpetual check** or repetition where the rules permit ([rules](/xiangqi/rules/)).
- Trade attackers, not defenders.
- Look for counter-attacks against a weak enemy palace.

## Practise

Endgames are best learned by playing them. Try short games against the Royal AI in [Xiangqi 3D](https://xiangqi3d.online) and, after each one, review the moment where material or the initiative changed hands.
