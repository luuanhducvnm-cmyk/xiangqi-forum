faq:
- Who moves first in xiangqi? | Red moves first.
- Can you castle in xiangqi? | No. There is no castling, no en passant and no promotion.
- Does stalemate mean a draw? | No. In xiangqi, a player with no legal moves loses.
- Can I jump over pieces? | Horses and elephants do not jump: they are blocked by pieces on their leg or eye. Only the cannon jumps, and only to capture, over exactly one piece.
- What is the difference between a chariot and a cannon? | Both slide along ranks and files. The chariot captures the first enemy piece in its path. The cannon captures only by jumping over exactly one piece, landing on the next piece.
- Can the generals face each other? | Never. Moves that leave them facing on an open file are illegal.
- Can soldiers move backward? | No. They only move forward, and sideways once across the river.
- How many pieces are there? | Sixteen per side, thirty-two in total.
- What is a good first move? | 1. C2=5, the Central Cannon, is the most popular. See the [openings guide](/xiangqi/openings/).
- Where can I play xiangqi online in 3D? | At [xiangqi3d.online](https://xiangqi3d.online) — play against real players, the Royal AI, or a friend with an invite link.
- How is a rating calculated? | Xiangqi ratings usually use the ELO system. See [how ELO works](/elo-rating-explained/).
- What is the best way to improve? | Play with a clock, review your games, learn tactics and a couple of openings, and study the [checkmate patterns](/xiangqi/checkmate-patterns/).
