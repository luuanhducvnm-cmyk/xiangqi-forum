faq:
- What is the Chinese word for xiangqi? | 象棋 (xiàngqí), literally "elephant chess" or "chess of the minister/elephant".
- How do you say checkmate in Chinese? | 將死 (jiàngsǐ) or 絕殺 (juéshā). A check is 將軍 (jiāngjūn), the word you will hear most often.

A quick reference for terms you will meet in books, videos and in the game. Translations of tactical terms vary between books and regions, so treat them as the most common usage rather than a single official standard. Chinese characters are shown in traditional form (Red's pieces use a slightly different character from Black's, so both are given for pieces).

## Pieces

| English | Chinese | Pinyin |
|---|---|---|
| General | 帥 (Red) / 將 (Black) | shuài / jiàng |
| Advisor (guard) | 仕 / 士 | shì |
| Elephant (minister) | 相 / 象 | xiàng |
| Horse (knight) | 傌 / 馬 | mǎ |
| Chariot (rook) | 俥 / 車 | jū (chē) |
| Cannon | 炮 / 砲 | pào |
| Soldier (pawn) | 兵 / 卒 | bīng / zú |
