Checkmate in xiangqi happens inside the palace. The general has few squares and the advisors and elephants have limited mobility, so mating patterns are short and repeatable. Here are three you can use today. **All three diagrams on this page are verified: the game's rules engine confirms that Black (the side to move) is checkmated.**

## 1. Double-chariot mate

Two chariots on adjacent ranks: one gives check along the back rank, the other covers the point in front of the general.

{{img:xq-mate-chariots|One chariot checks along Black's back rank; the other seals the second rank. The general has no escape.}}

**Why it works:** the first chariot attacks the whole back rank (including the points beside the general), and the second chariot attacks every point on the second rank. The general cannot move to any adjacent point, nothing can block, and nothing can capture either chariot.

**How to use it:** get both chariots onto open ranks. Trade off the enemy chariots first, and watch out for advisors that can interpose.

## 2. Chariot and horse

The chariot checks along the back rank; the horse covers the single point in front of the general.

{{img:xq-mate-horse-chariot|The chariot checks along the back rank; the horse, standing on the soldier rank, covers the only escape point in front of the general.}}

**Why it works:** the general cannot move sideways (covered by the chariot along the rank), cannot move forward (the horse attacks that point) and cannot capture anything. A horse is the perfect partner because it covers points no other piece can.

**Watch out for:** blocking the horse's leg. If an enemy piece stands next to the horse, the cover disappears.

## 3. Horse and cannon

The horse gives check from the second rank; the cannon covers the general's last flight square along the same rank.

{{img:xq-mate-cannon-horse|The horse gives check; the cannon behind it (through the horse as a screen) covers the point in front of the general. Advisors and elephants cannot help.}}

**Why it works:** even with both advisors and both elephants at home, the general cannot escape. The horse's check cannot be blocked at its leg by the defenders, and the cannon covers the one available flight square by firing through the horse as its screen.

## Building a mating net

You rarely get a position like the diagrams without preparation. The usual steps:

1. **Remove or deflect defenders.** Trade advisors or elephants, or force them away from the palace.
2. **Bring heavy pieces close.** Chariots to the back rank, horses to the palace flank.
3. **Cover the flight squares.** Each point next to the general must be attacked or blocked.
4. **Check last.** Give the final check only when every escape is covered.

## Defence: how to avoid being mated

- Keep your advisors and elephants as long as possible.
- Keep your own chariots active, so that they can capture a back-rank intruder.
- Do not let enemy horses reach the palace squares; keep soldiers or elephants controlling them.
- Always count the flight squares of your general before making a move.
