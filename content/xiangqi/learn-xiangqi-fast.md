You do not need months of study to start enjoying xiangqi. This plan takes about 30 to 45 minutes a day for one week and gets you from the rules to your first games against real opponents. It is a plan, not a promise: how fast you improve depends on how much you play and review.

## Day 1: the rules and the board (30 minutes)

Read [how to play xiangqi](/xiangqi/how-to-play/) and the [board and setup](/xiangqi/board-setup/) page. Your only goal is to be able to set up the board and name the seven kinds of piece. Do not try to learn strategy yet.

## Day 2: the pieces (40 minutes)

Study how each piece moves on the [pieces page](/xiangqi/pieces/). Pay most attention to the three pieces that surprise newcomers: the [horse](/xiangqi/pieces/horse/) (it can be hobbled), the [elephant](/xiangqi/pieces/elephant/) (it can be blocked at the elephant eye) and the [cannon](/xiangqi/pieces/cannon/) (it captures by jumping a screen). Then play one game against the computer just to practise moving.

## Day 3: the special rules (30 minutes)

Read the [complete rules](/xiangqi/rules/), the [flying general rule](/xiangqi/flying-general-rule/) and [stalemate in xiangqi](/xiangqi/stalemate/). These are the three places where xiangqi differs most from chess, and they decide many games.

## Day 4: basic strategy (40 minutes)

Read [beginner strategy](/xiangqi/strategy/) and the [piece values](/xiangqi/piece-values/) chart. The ideas to take away are: free the chariots, keep the general's palace solid, and do not push soldiers without a plan. Play two games and try to apply them.

## Day 5: tactics (45 minutes)

Read the [tactics page](/xiangqi/tactics/): forks, pins, discovered attacks and sacrifices. After each game, find one moment where you could have made a double attack. This single habit improves results faster than any opening theory.

## Day 6: one opening for each colour (40 minutes)

Pick one simple opening and learn only its first five moves. A good pair is the Central Cannon as Red and the Screen Horse as Black ([openings](/xiangqi/openings/)). Learn to read the moves with the [notation guide](/xiangqi/notation/). Play two games using the opening and see how your opponent replies.

## Day 7: finishing games (45 minutes)

Study the three standard [checkmate patterns](/xiangqi/checkmate-patterns/): two chariots, chariot and horse, and horse and cannon. Then play your first games against real opponents. Do not worry about your rating: the first games are for learning.

## After the first week

- **Play regularly.** Ten minutes a day beats three hours on one day.
- **Review your losses.** Find the move where the game turned and ask what you missed.
- **Learn endgames next.** Read the [endgame principles](/xiangqi/endgames/), then add a second opening.
- **Read the glossary** when a term confuses you ([glossary](/xiangqi/glossary/)).
