Strategy is the plan behind your moves. You do not need a deep theory to play xiangqi well — a few principles take you a long way.

## Opening: build your army

1. **Free the chariots.** They are your strongest pieces and begin trapped in the corners ([chariot page](/xiangqi/pieces/chariot/)). Move the horse or cannon in front of them to open a line.
2. **Develop the horses toward the centre**, and make sure their legs are not blocked ([horse page](/xiangqi/pieces/horse/)).
3. **Use cannons with screens.** A cannon with nothing to jump over is just a slow chariot ([cannon page](/xiangqi/pieces/cannon/)).
4. **Do not rush soldiers.** Soldiers cannot return, so each push is a commitment.
5. **Keep advisors and elephants at home** until you have a reason to move them.

For concrete first moves see [openings](/xiangqi/openings/).

## Middlegame: initiative and open files

- **Tempo and initiative.** Making threats forces the opponent to respond; many xiangqi games are won by whoever keeps making threats.
- **Control open files.** A chariot on an open file ties the opponent's pieces down.
- **Coordinate your pieces.** Chariot + cannon, horse + cannon and horse + chariot are the standard combinations ([tactics](/xiangqi/tactics/)).
- **Attack the weakest point.** Look at the opponent's palace: missing advisors or elephants usually give a target.
- **Keep your pieces protected.** An unprotected piece is a target for forks.

## Trading pieces

A quick point chart is on the [piece values](/xiangqi/piece-values/) page.

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| You are ahead in material | Trade pieces, especially chariots, to simplify |
| You are behind | Avoid trades; keep pieces for counterplay |
| Your general is exposed | Trade off the opponent's attackers |
| Opponent's general is exposed | Keep your attackers; trade defenders |

Trade the opponent's strong pieces for your weaker ones when you can.

## The general's safety

- Keep at least one advisor and one elephant until the endgame.
- Watch the **flying-general** line: do not let your general and the enemy's face each other with only a piece between ([rules](/xiangqi/rules/)).
- Respect a cannon on your general's file: if exactly one piece stands between it and your general, you are in check; if two pieces stand between, moving either one away can suddenly give check.
- Count the legal escapes for your general; if the number gets low, expect a mate.

## Common beginner mistakes

1. Leaving chariots in the corners.
2. Moving soldiers forward for no reason.
3. Forgetting the horse leg and elephant eye ([hobbling](/xiangqi/pieces/horse/)).
4. Making a cannon move without a screen.
5. Ignoring the opponent's threats; always check "what does the last move threaten?".
6. Trading a chariot for a horse or cannon.
7. Exposing the general to the flying-general rule.
8. Running out of time: practise with a clock.

## Endgame strategy in brief

In the endgame the soldiers and the general become important: advance connected soldiers, bring the general to a central palace point, and use chariots to cut the enemy general off. Many endgames that look like a clear extra piece are drawn if the defender keeps advisors and elephants — see [endgame principles](/xiangqi/endgames/).

## A simple improvement plan

- **Play** 3–5 games per week with a clock.
- **Review** each loss: find the move where it went wrong.
- **Learn** one opening system and one tactical theme per week.
- **Practise** checkmate patterns until you see them instantly ([checkmate patterns](/xiangqi/checkmate-patterns/)).
- **Track your rating**: [how ELO works](/elo-rating-explained/) and how it is used in Xiangqi 3D.
