*Updated 19 November 2026.*

On **27 September 2025**, Vietnamese grandmaster **Lai Ly Huynh** won the men's individual standard final of the **World Xiangqi Championship** in Shanghai, beating China's **Yin Sheng**. VnExpress reports that China had won every edition of the event since the championship began in 1990, so the result ended a three-decade run of Chinese winners.

## The final

- Lai played Black. VnExpress describes Yin, then 20, as a prodigy who opened with a double-cannon attack but lost a horse after a miscalculation in the middlegame.
- Lai captured the horse, later traded a cannon for a key advisor and pushed his soldiers in the endgame. After nearly two hours Yin resigned.
- The title was decided in the standard games, without the rapid or blitz tiebreaks, Nhan Dan reports.
- Vietnam had lost two earlier finals in this event: Nguyen Thanh Bao in 2009 and Lai himself in 2023, when he lost the final on tiebreaks to Meng Chen.

## Who is Lai Ly Huynh?

According to Wikipedia he was born on 16 May 1990 in Vinh Long, holds the grandmaster title and is a Vietnamese national champion. His earlier results include gold in the Open Rapid and the men's team events at the 2022 World Championship in Kuching, another Open Rapid gold at the 2023 World Championship in Houston, and gold in men's standard singles at the 2023 SEA Games in Phnom Penh. His personal best rating was 2500 in January 2020 and his peak world ranking was 43rd in July 2021.
