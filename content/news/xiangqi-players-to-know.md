*Updated 19 November 2026. We list only players whose results we found in the sources below, and we do not give rating numbers because we found no single authoritative current list.*

## Lai Ly Huynh (Vietnam)

World Xiangqi Championship men's standard winner in **2025**, grandmaster, born in Vinh Long. He ended a three-decade run of Chinese winners; read [the story](/news/lai-ly-huynh-world-xiangqi-champion/). In 2026 he was runner-up at the Wuyang Cup finals; see [his 2026 season](/news/lai-ly-huynh-2026-season/).

## Yin Sheng (China)

Lost the 2025 world final to Lai Ly Huynh. VnExpress described him as a 20-year-old prodigy who opened the final with a double-cannon attack.

## Meng Chen (China)

Won the 2023 world championship final on tiebreaks against Lai Ly Huynh, according to Wikipedia's biography of Lai.

## Cheng Yudong (China)

Won the 2026 Wuyang Cup finals in Guangzhou, beating Lai Ly Huynh in the second leg of the final, according to Vietnam's sports authority.

## Wang Yubo and Cao Yanlei (China)

At the 2026 Xuanqiu Dayebei, Tuoi Tre reported that Lai lost twice to each of them. Wang Yubo is described there as the reigning Chinese champion.

## Nguyen Thanh Bao (Vietnam)

Reached the men's standard world final in 2009, one of Vietnam's two finalists before Lai's win, according to VnExpress.
