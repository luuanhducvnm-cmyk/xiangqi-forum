*Updated 19 November 2026.*

A note on the name: the "Chinese Chess Championships" reported here is China's national championship in **international chess** (the western game). The traditional Chinese game, xiangqi, which many people also call "Chinese chess", has separate national events. See [xiangqi versus chess](/xiangqi/vs-chess/) for the difference.

## Results

The 11-round championship ended in Xinghua on 18 May 2026.

| Section | Champion | Score | Runner-up / podium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open | Xu Xiangyu (Chengdu Chess Academy) | 8½/11 | Kong Xiangrui second on tiebreak (8), Xu Yinglun also 8 |
| Women | Yan Tianqi (Hangzhou Mind Sports Team) | 8½/11 | Chen Yining second (8), Wang Qinxuanyi third (7½) |

According to ChessBase, both champions earned places on China's teams for the 2026 Chess Olympiad, and FIDE's headline says the same.

## What happened next

At the Olympiad in Samarkand in September, China's women won gold, while the men finished eighth. Read the full [Olympiad report](/news/chess-olympiad-2026-samarkand/). China's Wei Yi is eighth in the world on the [November FIDE list](/news/fide-rating-list-november-2026/).
