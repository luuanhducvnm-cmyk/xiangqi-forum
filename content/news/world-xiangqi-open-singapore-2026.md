*Updated 19 November 2026. At the time of writing we could not find published final standings, so this article covers the event rules and format only; check the [WXF](https://www.wxf-xiangqi.org/) or the organiser site for results.*

The **5th World Xiangqi Open**, part of the 2026 World Xiangqi Series, took place in **Singapore** in November 2026. It is organised by the **World Xiangqi Federation (WXF)** and **SIXGA**, the Singapore Xiangqi General Association.

## Key facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Dates | Competition 10-13 November 2026 (check-in 8-9 November, opening ceremony 9 November) |
| Venue | Singapore Chinese Cultural Centre, 1 Straits Boulevard |
| Rules | World Xiangqi Rules |
| Time control | 40 minutes + 20 seconds per move |
| Rounds | Seven |
| Events | Men's team, women's team, U18 men, U18 women |
| Teams | Men's teams of 3 fixed boards, women's teams of 2 |
| Entries | 16 countries and regions: 22 men's teams, 8 women's teams, 36 U18 players (entry list of 23 October, subject to change) |
| Prizes | Listed cash prizes total US$6,900: men's team US$2,000 / 1,500 / 1,000, women's team US$1,000 / 800 / 600 |

The organiser's page lists "10-13 November" in one place and "10-15 November" in its footer, so treat the exact end date as to be confirmed.

## Why a team event?

Most world titles in xiangqi are individual, but the Open uses team formats so that national federations can send full squads, including juniors. For readers new to the game, the rules are summarised in [how to play xiangqi](/xiangqi/how-to-play/), and one rule that often decides games is covered in [perpetual check and repetition](/xiangqi/perpetual-check-and-repetition/).
