*Updated 19 November 2026. All details come from the sources below; we could not verify full game records.*

After becoming world champion in September 2025 (see [how it happened](/news/lai-ly-huynh-world-xiangqi-champion/)), Lai Ly Huynh played a season of strong Chinese professional events. Results were mixed.

## March: runner-up at the Wuyang Cup finals

At the 2026 Wuyang Cup finals in Guangzhou (reported 1 March 2026), **Cheng Yudong** won the title and beat Lai in the second leg of the final. Vietnam's sports authority reports that Lai finished as runner-up after playing 10 games with 4 wins, 5 draws and 1 loss over the tournament. Bao Van Hoa reported that he got past Chinese masters to reach the final.

## May and June: a winless run at Xuanqiu Dayebei

In season 2 of the Xuanqiu Dayebei, a Chinese tournament with a prize fund of up to 1.2 million yuan, Lai lost all four of his games and was eliminated, Tuoi Tre reported on 2 June 2026. He lost twice to **Cao Yanlei** and twice to **Wang Yubo**, the reigning Chinese champion. The last game was the closest: Lai got a big middlegame advantage but made errors in the endgame. Chinese commentators expressed surprise and sympathy rather than criticism and suggested psychological factors or unfamiliarity with the format as possible reasons.

## Domestic championship

Bao Van Hoa headlined that Lai stopped at the quarter-finals of the 2026 Vietnamese national championship. We have not been able to read the full report, so we do not include scores or opponents.

## What this tells us

Elite xiangqi is extremely tight. A world champion can lead a tournament one month and lose every game in the next, because the best Chinese professionals prepare deeply and play a huge number of games.
