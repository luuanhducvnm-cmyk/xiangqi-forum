*Updated 19 November 2026. We report only what the sources below state; we have not seen the association's full notice.*

The **Chinese Xiangqi Association** announced a new round of penalties on **13 April 2026**, according to Caixin's report published the next day. Three grandmasters received **lifetime bans**, lost their technical titles and were permanently barred from taking part in or organising events under the association and its affiliated units.

## Context

The sanctions are part of an integrity scandal in Chinese professional xiangqi that began with leaked audio recordings, which later came to be known as "Recording Gate". Chess.com has reported earlier lifetime bans on top Chinese players for match-fixing and bribery, and the General Administration of Sport of China announced that 41 players had been penalized. The details and the legal aspects of individual cases are for the authorities to describe, and we do not repeat allegations beyond what these outlets reported.

## What it means for fans

Xiangqi continues to be played at the top level in China and abroad, for example at the [World Xiangqi Open](/news/world-xiangqi-open-singapore-2026/) in Singapore and in Vietnam, where world champion [Lai Ly Huynh](/news/lai-ly-huynh-world-xiangqi-champion/) is based. The rules of the game, including how [perpetual check](/xiangqi/perpetual-check-and-repetition/) is judged, have not changed.
