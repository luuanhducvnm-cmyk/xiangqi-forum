*Updated 19 November 2026.*

The **46th Chess Olympiad** finished in Samarkand, Uzbekistan, on 27 September 2026. The home team **Uzbekistan** won the open section and **China** won the women's section, according to FIDE and Xinhua. Nearly 2,000 players from about 400 teams took part in the 11-round Swiss event, Xinhua reports.

## Final results

| Section | Gold | Silver | Bronze |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open | Uzbekistan | India | Germany |
| Women | China | Kazakhstan | India (on tiebreak) |

## How the last day went

- **Open:** Uzbekistan beat Ukraine 2.5-1.5 in the final round on home soil, finishing with 10 wins and 1 loss (20 match points) per Xinhua. India secured silver with a 2-2 draw against Hungary, and Germany took bronze by beating the Netherlands 2.5-1.5.
- **Women:** China beat Vietnam 3-1 in the final round to finish alone in first place with 9 wins and 2 draws (20 points). It was China's seventh women's Olympiad title, Xinhua notes. Kazakhstan took silver after drawing with Poland, and India won bronze on tiebreak after a 2-2 draw with Georgia.

## Individual medals

Wei Yi won the gold medal on second board in the open section, and Zhu Jinner (first board) and Lu Miaoyi (third board) won women's board golds, according to Xinhua. China's men lost to Ukraine in round 10 and finished eighth.

## Why it matters

The Olympiad is the biggest team event in chess, and national teams are chosen from the strongest players of each country. Several of the players above also appear in the [FIDE top 10](/news/fide-rating-list-november-2026/), and China's team was built from the national championship covered in [our report on the 2026 Chinese Chess Championships](/news/china-chess-championship-2026/).
