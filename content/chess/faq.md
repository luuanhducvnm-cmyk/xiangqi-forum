faq:
- Who moves first in chess? | White.
- What is the goal of chess? | To checkmate the opponent's king.
- What is the strongest piece? | The queen.
- Can a pawn capture forward? | No. Pawns move straight but capture diagonally.
- What is castling? | A special move in which the king moves two squares toward a rook and the rook jumps to the other side. See [special moves](/chess/special-moves/).
- What is stalemate? | The player to move has no legal moves and is not in check. It is a draw.
- How is a chess game drawn? | By stalemate, insufficient material, threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule or agreement. See [how games end](/chess/ways-to-end/).
- What is the best opening for beginners? | 1.e4 with sound development, or the London System as White; 1...e5 or the Caro-Kann as Black. See [openings](/chess/openings/).
- What does ELO mean? | A rating system that estimates player strength. See [ELO ratings explained](/elo-rating-explained/).
- What does 10+5 mean? | 10 minutes per player plus a 5-second increment per move. See [time controls](/chess/time-controls/).
- Where can I play chess online in 3D? | At [chess3d.online](https://chess3d.online), against real players, against the Royal AI, or against a friend with an invite link.
- Is chess the same as xiangqi? | No — xiangqi is Chinese chess, a related game with a different board, a river, palaces and a cannon. See [xiangqi vs chess](/xiangqi/vs-chess/).
