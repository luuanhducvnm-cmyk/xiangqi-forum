Opening traps teach tactics. Each of the three below is **forced from the position shown, and every move was verified by the Chess 3D rules engine**, including the final checkmate or material count.

## 1. Légal's Mate

**1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 d6 3.Bc4 Bg4 4.Nc3 g6 5.Nxe5 Bxd1 6.Bxf7+ Ke7 7.Nd5#**

White offers the queen. If Black takes it with 5...Bxd1?, White checks with the bishop, and the king is mated by the knight.

{{img:ln-legal|Légal's mate: 7.Nd5# after White sacrificed the queen.}}

**The lesson:** capturing a queen is not automatically correct. As Black, check what the capture allows: here a check on f7 and a mating knight jump. As White, remember the idea whenever an opponent pins your knight with ...Bg4 and takes a pawn on e5 for granted.

## 2. Blackburne Shilling Gambit

**1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4 Nd4 4.Nxe5? Qg5 5.Nxf7? Qxg2 6.Rf1 Qxe4+ 7.Be2 Nf3#**

Black sets a trap with 3...Nd4. If White grabs pawns with 4.Nxe5 and 5.Nxf7, Black's queen takes g2 and e4, and the knight delivers checkmate.

{{img:ln-shilling|Blackburne Shilling Gambit: 7...Nf3# checkmates the white king.}}

**How to avoid it:** simply capture the knight: **4.Nxd4 exd4**, and the trap is avoided. The lesson is that taking the "free" pawns is not always best.

## 3. The Elephant Trap

**1.d4 d5 2.c4 e6 3.Nc3 Nf6 4.Bg5 Nbd7 5.cxd5 exd5 6.Nxd5? Nxd5 7.Bxd8 Bb4+ 8.Qd2 Bxd2+ 9.Kxd2 Kxd8**

White wins a pawn on d5 and a queen on d8, but ends up a piece down: Black's check on b4 forces the queens off and takes back the bishop. At the end Black has won a knight for a pawn.

{{img:ln-elephant|Elephant Trap: after 9...Kxd8 Black has won a piece for a pawn.}}

**How to avoid it:** do not grab the pawn on d5 here. After 6.Nxd5 the knight has left c3, so ...Bb4+ becomes possible and Black wins material back with interest.

## The general lesson

1. A "free" pawn or piece is often a bait.
2. Always check forcing moves (checks, captures and threats) for your opponent after any capture.
3. Practise with [mate-in-2 puzzles](/chess/puzzles/mate-in-2/) to improve your tactical vision.

For more ideas see the [Italian Game](/chess/italian-game/) (the home of Légal's mate) and the [tactics guide](/chess/tactics/), and try them on [Chess 3D](https://chess3d.online).
