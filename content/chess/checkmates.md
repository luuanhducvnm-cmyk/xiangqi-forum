Checkmate patterns are the targets of the whole game. Here are five you should recognise instantly. **Every diagram on this page has been verified by the game's rules engine: the side to move really is checkmated.**

## 1. Fool's mate

The fastest possible checkmate: **1.f3 e5 2.g4 Qh4#**. White opens the diagonal to his own king by moving the f- and g-pawns.

{{img:ch-fools-mate|Fool's mate: Black's queen on h4 checks the king and there is no escape.}}

You will not see it against a good opponent, but it shows how dangerous the e1–h4 diagonal is.

## 2. Scholar's mate

A four-move trap: **1.e4 e5 2.Qh5 Nc6 3.Bc4 Nf6?? 4.Qxf7#**. The queen and bishop target the weak f7 square (protected only by the king).

{{img:ch-scholars-mate|Scholar's mate: the queen on f7, protected by the bishop on c4, is checkmate.}}

**Defence:** after 1.e4 e5 2.Qh5 Nc6 3.Bc4, do **not** play 3...Nf6?? (that is the mistake that allows the mate). Play 3...g6, which attacks the queen, or 3...Qe7, which protects f7. Always ask whether the queen and bishop together attack f7.

## 3. Back-rank mate

A rook (or queen) checks along the back rank while the king is boxed in by its own pawns.

{{img:ch-back-rank|Back-rank mate: the rook on a8 gives checkmate; the black king is trapped by its own pawns.}}

**Defence:** make an escape square ("luft") with a pawn move such as h6, and keep a rook on the back rank.

## 4. Smothered mate

A knight checks a king that is completely surrounded by its own pieces.

{{img:ch-smothered|Smothered mate: Nf7# with Black's king, rook and pawns blocking each other.}}

The classic way to reach it is a queen sacrifice that forces the rook to take: Qg8+ Rxg8 and then Nf7#.

## General mating principles

- **Check last.** Give a check only when it forces a win or you have calculated the line.
- **Remove the defenders.** Capture or deflect the pieces guarding the king.
- **Cut off escape squares.** A king with no flight squares is easy to mate.
- **Count the squares.** Before you attack, count what the king can do.

## How to avoid being checkmated

- Castle early and keep pawns in front of the king.
- Do not move pawns around the king without a reason.
- Look at every opponent's check and see what it threatens.
- Make "luft" for your king in the middlegame.
