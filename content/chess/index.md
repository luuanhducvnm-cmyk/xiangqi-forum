Chess is the world's most popular strategy game: two players, 16 pieces each, and one goal — checkmate the opponent's king. These guides take you from the first move to your first tactics, with diagrams generated from the same rules engine that powers [Chess 3D](https://chess3d.online).

## Chess at a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Players | 2 (White moves first, Black second) |
| Board | 8×8 squares, 64 in total |
| Pieces per side | 16: 1 king, 1 queen, 2 rooks, 2 bishops, 2 knights, 8 pawns |
| Goal | Checkmate the opponent's king |
| Special moves | Castling, en passant, pawn promotion |
| Ways to draw | Stalemate, repetition, 50-move rule, insufficient material, agreement |
