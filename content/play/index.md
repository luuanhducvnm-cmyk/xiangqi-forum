Two free browser games, one idea: classic board games on a real-feeling 3D board, with a two-button clock like the ones in tournaments.

{{cta:both}}

## What you get

- **A 3D board** you can rotate, with a camera that fits phones and desktops.
- **A real two-button clock**: make your move, then press your side's button. Switch on the auto-clock option if you prefer your move to end your turn automatically.
- **Royal AI** for practice games at any time.
- **Online matches** with players of similar rating, or with a friend through an **invite link**.
- **ELO ratings and ranks** — everyone starts at 1000; see [how ELO works](/elo-rating-explained/).
- **Draw offers**, resignation, reconnecting if your connection drops, and automatic draws by rule.
- **Install as an app** on your phone (Add to Home Screen).
