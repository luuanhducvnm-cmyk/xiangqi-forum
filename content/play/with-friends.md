Playing a friend is one of the best parts of online chess. Both [Chess 3D](https://chess3d.online) and [Xiangqi 3D](https://xiangqi3d.online) have a one-tap **invite link**.

{{cta:both}}

## How it works

1. **Open the game** and choose a name if you have not already.
2. **Tap the invite button.** The game creates a link for you.
3. **Share the link** by message, email or any chat app.
4. **Keep the page open.** The link is valid for 30 minutes while you stay in the lobby.
5. **Your friend opens the link,** picks a name, and the game starts.

## What kind of game is it?

- A **normal rated game** with a 10 minutes + 5 seconds per move clock.
- It counts **even if your ratings are far apart** — you are not matched by rating when you invite.
- To keep ratings fair, **the number of games between the same two players** is limited per hour and per day.
- You can **offer draws**; a draw by agreement changes nobody's rating.

## Tips

- If your friend is new, play a few practice games against the [Royal AI](/play/vs-computer/) together, or send them the beginner guides: [how to play chess](/chess/how-to-play/) or [how to play xiangqi](/xiangqi/how-to-play/).
- The game assigns the sides, so you do not need to agree on colours in advance.
- If the link has expired, just create a new one.
- Wondering how the numbers move? See [ELO ratings explained](/elo-rating-explained/).
