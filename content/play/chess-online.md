**[Chess 3D](https://chess3d.online)** is a browser chess game with a 3D board, a real two-button chess clock and an ELO ladder.

{{cta:chess}}

## Features

- **3D board and pieces** — rotate the view; the camera fits both phones and desktops.
- **Real two-button clock** — make your move, then press your side of the clock. Switch on the auto-clock if you want your move to end your turn.
- **Royal AI** — a practice opponent that is always available.
- **Online matchmaking** — the lobby pairs you with players of similar rating.
- **Invite links** — play a friend by sending a link.
- **ELO ranks** — start at 1000; tiers run from Pawn through Knight, Bishop, Rook, Queen and King to Grandmaster ([how ELO works](/elo-rating-explained/)).
- **Draw offers** — offer a draw after the first two moves; a draw by agreement changes nobody's rating.
- **Full rules** — castling, en passant, promotion with a piece chooser, stalemate, insufficient material, threefold repetition and the fifty-move rule detected automatically.
- **Reconnect support** and an inactivity timer, so a game never hangs.
- **Legal-move hints** — tap a piece to see where it can go.

## Controls

- Tap a piece, then tap a destination square.
- Tap the moved piece again to change the move, or use the undo button.
- Press your side of the clock to confirm the move, or enable auto-clock.
- Top-left buttons: resign, draw offer, auto-clock, sound and view.

## Time control

Games use **10 minutes plus 5 seconds per move** — a rapid format that gives you time to think. See [chess time controls](/chess/time-controls/) for how increments work.
