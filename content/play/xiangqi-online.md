**[Xiangqi 3D](https://xiangqi3d.online)** is a browser game that brings Chinese chess to a 3D board with a real two-button clock.

{{cta:xiangqi}}

## Features

- **3D board and pieces** — rotate the view; the board turns so you are always at the bottom.
- **Real two-button clock** — move first, then press your clock button. Optional auto-clock if you prefer.
- **Royal AI** — play at any time to learn or warm up.
- **Online matchmaking** — the lobby pairs you with players of similar rating.
- **Invite links** — play with a friend: send a link, and the game begins when they join.
- **ELO ranks** — start at 1000; ranks run from Soldier to General ([how ELO works](/elo-rating-explained/)).
- **Draw offers** — offer or accept a draw; a draw by agreement changes nobody's rating.
- **Fair-play rules** — perpetual check and chase rules, an inactivity timer, reconnect support.
- **Legal-move hints** — tap a piece and its legal destinations are shown.
- **English and Chinese interface.**

## New to xiangqi?

You will be up and running in minutes:

1. Read [how to play xiangqi](/xiangqi/how-to-play/).
2. Learn the [seven pieces](/xiangqi/pieces/).
3. Play a few games against the Royal AI.
4. Try the [openings](/xiangqi/openings/) in games against people.

## Controls

- Tap a piece to select it, then tap a destination. The move is shown on the board.
- Tap the moved piece again to take it back, or use the undo button.
- Press your side of the clock to confirm the move (or enable auto-clock).
- Buttons in the top-left corner: resign, draw offer, auto-clock, sound and view.

## Tips for your first rated games

- Play at least ten rated games to appear on the leaderboard.
- Games count only after at least ten moves.
- Rated games use 10 minutes + 5 seconds per move.

Read [xiangqi strategy for beginners](/xiangqi/strategy/) before your first rated game, and [Xiangqi 3D](https://xiangqi3d.online) will do the rest.
